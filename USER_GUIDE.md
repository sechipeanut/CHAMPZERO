## 1. Getting Started (Account & Profile Setup)

### 🔑 Registration & Login
1. Click **LOGIN / REGISTER** on the top right navigation bar.
2. Sign in using **Google Single Sign-On** or your **Email & Password**.
3. Once logged in, your tactical profile dropdown will appear on the top right with your avatar and badge.

### 🎮 Customizing Your In-Game Identities (Big 3 Titles)
Navigate to [**/edit-profile**](file:///c:/Users/sean/Downloads/Website-20250714T082128Z-1-001/Website/CHAMPZERO/edit-profile.html) to link your competitive game stats:
- **In-Game Name (IGN)**: Your primary platform handle.
- **Valorant (PC)**: Enter your Riot ID (`Name#Tag`), competitive rank (*Iron 1 through Radiant*), and main agent role (*Duelist, Initiator, Controller, Sentinel, Flex*).
- **Mobile Legends (MLBB)**: Enter your MLBB User ID & Server Zone, competitive rank (*Warrior through Mythical Immortal*), and lane position (*Gold Lane, Exp Lane, Mid Lane, Jungler, Roamer*).
- **Honor of Kings (HOK)**: Enter your Player ID, official position role (*Clash Lane, Farm Lane, Mid Lane, Jungler, Roamer, Flex*), and rank tier (*Bronze through Legend / Legend King*).
- **Avatar Upload**: Click the profile picture upload button to choose a custom squad or player avatar.
- Click **SAVE PROFILE CHANGES**.

## 2. Player & Team Guide

### 🏆 Joining Tournaments
Navigate to [**/tournaments**](file:///c:/Users/sean/Downloads/Website-20250714T082128Z-1-001/Website/CHAMPZERO/tournaments.html):
- **Team Squad Registration**:
  1. Click **REGISTER TEAM** on any upcoming tournament.
  2. Select your registered team from the dropdown.
  3. If there is an entry fee, you will be redirected to the secure **PayRex Checkout** (GCash, Maya, Cards, or Online Banking).
  4. Your team will appear under the *Registered Teams* list immediately upon payment confirmation.
- **Solo Queue Automated Draft**:
  1. If you don't have a team, click **JOIN SOLO QUEUE**.
  2. Select your preferred in-game role (e.g. *Duelist*, *Jungler*, *Roamer*).
  3. ChampZero automatically bundles solo players into balanced tournament draft rosters before the bracket seeds!

### ⚔️ Brackets & Match Reporting
1. When the tournament starts, open the interactive **Bracket Tree** to find your match matchup.
2. Click on your match node to enter the **Match Room**.
3. Both team captains connect in the private match chat to coordinate game lobby codes.
4. **Submitting Match Results**:
   - Team Captains enter the final score (e.g., `2 - 1`).
   - Click **Upload Screenshot Proof** to attach the post-game victory scoreboard.
   - Click **Submit Score**.
   - If both captains submit matching scores, the system automatically advances the winning team to the next bracket round!

### 💰 Prize Pool Claims & Payouts
1. If your team finishes on the winner's podium (*1st, 2nd, or 3rd Place*), a **Claim Payout** button appears on your tournament dashboard.
2. Enter your verified payment method: **GCash**, **Maya**, or **Bank Account Name and Number**.
3. The tournament organizer verifies the match records and disburses your prize share directly, uploading a receipt proof to your claim ticket.

### 👥 Creating Teams & Finding Free Agents
Navigate to [**/teams**](file:///c:/Users/sean/Downloads/Website-20250714T082128Z-1-001/Website/CHAMPZERO/teams.html):
- **Create a Squad**:
  - Click **CREATE TEAM**, choose your game title (*Valorant, Mobile Legends, or Honor of Kings*), team name, tag (e.g. `CZ`), and logo.
  - As Captain, use the **Invite Player** button to send roster invites by IGN.
- **Free Agents Directory**:
  - Filter players by Game (*Valorant, MLBB, HOK*), Rank Tier, and Role.
  - Click **Toggle Looking For Team (LFT)** to advertise your profile on the free agent board.

### 🟢 Hosting & Challenging Scrims
- **Post a Scrim Lobby**:
  - Click **POST OPEN SCRIM** on the Scrims tab.
  - Select your game (*Valorant, MLBB, or HOK*), target Rank Range, Format (BO1/BO3), and Scheduled Time.
  - The scrim lobby immediately lights up **Emerald Green** (`LOBBY OPEN`) on the public board.
- **Challenging a Scrim**:
  - Browse open lobbies and click **CHALLENGE**.
  - The host captain receives an instant notification to accept and open match coordination chat.

## 5. Organizer Guide

Navigate to [**/organizer**](file:///c:/Users/sean/Downloads/Website-20250714T082128Z-1-001/Website/CHAMPZERO/organizer.html):

### 🛠️ Creating Tournaments & Prize Escrow
1. Click **CREATE TOURNAMENT**.
2. Select your game (*Valorant, Mobile Legends: Bang Bang, or Honor of Kings*), Tournament Title, Format (*5v5 Single Elimination / Double Elimination*), Maximum Team Cap, and Rules.
3. Set the **Prize Pool Amount** and **Entry Fee**.
4. **Fund Prize Escrow**: Click **Fund Prize Pool** to deposit the escrow via PayRex checkout. This marks your tournament with a verified **Escrow Secured** badge to build player trust!

### 📊 Bracket Management & Dispute Resolution
1. Once registrations close, click **Generate Brackets**.
2. The system seeds teams and generates the interactive bracket.
3. If two captains submit conflicting match scores, the match is flagged as **DISPUTED**:
   - Open the Dispute Review modal.
   - Inspect screenshot scoreboard proofs uploaded by both captains.
   - Click **Confirm Winner** on the rightful team to advance them.

### 💸 Prize Disbursement
1. Review winner claim tickets under the **Payout Claims** tab.
2. Send the prize share to the winner's GCash / Maya / Bank.
3. Upload the transfer transaction receipt and click **Confirm Disbursement**.

Navigate to [**/admin**](file:///c:/Users/sean/Downloads/Website-20250714T082128Z-1-001/Website/CHAMPZERO/admin.html):

- Switch to the **Supporters & Wall** tab.

## 7. FAQ & Troubleshooting

**Q: Which games are supported on ChampZero?**  
*A: ChampZero is exclusively tailored for **Valorant (PC)**, **Mobile Legends: Bang Bang (MLBB)**, and **Honor of Kings (HOK)**.*

**Q: Can I join a tournament without a team?**  
*A: Yes! Use the **Solo Queue** button on the tournament card and select your main role. ChampZero drafts solo players into balanced squads automatically.*

**Q: What payment methods are supported for Entry Fees and Supporter Badges?**  
*A: We support **GCash**, **Maya**, **Credit/Debit Cards (Visa/Mastercard)**, and **Online Bank Transfers** processed securely through PayRex.*

**Q: How do I know when my supporter badge expires?**  
*A: Your active remaining days are displayed on the Wall of Fame and in your Profile banner (e.g. `Active Backer • 28d left`). You can renew or upgrade anytime to extend your perks.*
